03 / Entertainment & awards

A spotlight
on the stories.

Disney awards campaign websites that brought films, campaign materials, and media together in one experience.

Project
2018 Guild & AMPAS campaign sites
Focus
Film navigation, responsive layouts & media interfaces
Format
Multi-film awards campaigns

The context

Awards campaigns have more to share than a trailer. Each film needs a home for its story, consideration materials, press, and media, within a site that lets visitors move between titles.

The front-end experience

The archived sites bring together a shared film menu and title-specific pages. Films including Black Panther, Incredibles 2, and Bao sit within a common navigation structure while keeping their own artwork and campaign content.

Navigation & responsive behavior

The Guild implementation tracks the selected film and page in the URL, allowing direct links into individual sections. Its layouts adapt between mobile and desktop, with film content and navigation arranged for the available space.

Media & campaign content

The archive includes film galleries, video interfaces, and score and song sections, alongside press and consideration materials. The available sections vary by title rather than forcing every film into an identical set of pages.

What makes it interesting

A shared front-end structure supports very different films. The interaction connects them; the imagery gives each title its own identity.

04 / Animation & interaction

Two shorts.
Two little worlds.

A playful awards campaign site for DreamWorks Animation’s Bilby and Bird Karma.

Project
DreamWorks Animation Shorts FYC 2018
Focus
Film selection, transitions & exploration
Content
Clips, photos & filmmaker profiles

The experience

The site starts with a choice: two film posters, each opening its own world. Selecting a short transforms the header into a film menu and reveals that film’s artwork and content.

The interaction details

The preserved front end uses a flipping header, film-to-film transitions, and photo selection to make browsing feel playful. Inside each film, visitors can explore clips, stills, and the people behind the story.

Keeping each film’s state in order

Switching films resets the selected subpage and gallery state. Photo thumbnails update the main image, filmmaker navigation switches profiles, and closing a media panel pauses and resets its videos.

Adapting the experience for mobile

The original implementation changes how film subpages open on smaller screens, using separate content views for the selected section. The same films and materials remain the center of the experience.

Why it belongs here

It brings together the parts of front-end work I enjoy: character, motion, and small interactions that invite someone to keep exploring.

The film switcher here previews recovered artwork. The original site’s full interactions will be featured separately.
